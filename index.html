About

I am a backend-leaning engineer who kept saying yes to the harder problem, and ended up leading the teams that solve them.

My work sits where product pressure meets systems reality: a video call that has to stay up while four parties legally sign a document, a search box that has to return in milliseconds across billions of records, an onboarding pipeline that used to take fifteen days and now takes three. I care about the unglamorous parts — caching strategy, queue semantics, the failure mode nobody wrote a ticket for.

Alongside the code, I have spent most of the last six years building people. I have mentored engineers across two distributed teams through planning, design, review and delivery, and I still think the best architecture decision is the one the whole team can explain back to you.

Outside work you will find me cooking my way through a cuisine I have not tried yet, tinkering with Linux — a love affair since 2013 — or heading for the mountains.
